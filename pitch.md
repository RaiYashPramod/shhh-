Hollow Manor Hide and Seek
Tagline: A spooky Halloween hide and seek in a haunted manor: one seeker hunts, everyone else hides, and footsteps are your only warning.

Pitch
Players share a big haunted manor made of connected rooms. One player is the Seeker, a pumpkin-headed stalker; the rest are trick-or-treat kids who hide. When the Seeker gets close, a rising footstep sound warns the hiders to slip through a door into the next room.

Run length: 5 minutes

Arc
Each round starts with 20 seconds of countdown while hiders scatter and the Seeker is blind at the start room. Early on the manor is quiet and hiders pick good spots. As the Seeker sweeps rooms, footsteps grow louder within 12 units and hiders must bolt through doors, which become the main tension. As kids get tagged the manor gets emptier and the lights flicker more. The round ends when everyone is found or the 5 minute clock runs out.

Moments
Hiding in a wardrobe as heavy footsteps stop right outside, then fade away.
Hearing the footsteps rise and bolting through a door just as the Seeker enters behind you.
Opening a door into a room where another hider is already crouched, both of you startled.
The Seeker's lantern swinging across a room as you hold your breath under a table.
The final hider outlasting the clock with a few seconds left.
Controls and feel
WASD to move, mouse to look, Shift to sneak, E to open doors or hide, click to tag (Seeker only).

Endings
Win: Hiders win if at least one survives until the 5 minute clock runs out; the Seeker wins by tagging every hider. Lose: A tagged hider becomes a spectator for the rest of the round and respawns at the start room next round. The Seeker loses if time runs out.

World and cast
Hollow Manor is a single large rectangular haunted house, 60 by 40 units, split into a 3 by 2 grid of rooms (a library, a kitchen, a ballroom, a nursery, a cellar, a greenhouse). Each room is 20 by 20 units, and open doors connect neighbors. Cobwebs, candles, pumpkins and old furniture fill the rooms, lit by warm orange glow against deep purple shadows.
